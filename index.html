How to read this report

Two kinds of evidence appear here, and they are not equal.

Verified numbers come from real Pokémon Showdown battles played under the current ruleset — the engine the game is actually played on. These are the numbers that count.

Practice sim numbers come from a fast approximate simulator the lab uses to scout habits: which four get picked, who leads, what moves open the game. Good for reading tendencies, but hypotheses only — a practice-sim number is never proof and is always labelled.

Confidence chips grade the verified sample: Strong evidence means 500+ verified games against 8+ different opponents; Established 200+ against 6+; Still developing 60+ against 3+; Early anything less. “Scouting probe out of date” means the habit data was recorded before the latest rules update — the verified record itself is always current.

A “game” is counted from this team’s side of a battle. “Picked” means chosen among the four Pokémon actually brought to a battle — teams register six, only four play.

How to read this report

Two kinds of evidence appear here, and they are not equal.

Verified numbers come from real Pokémon Showdown battles played under the current ruleset — the engine the game is actually played on. These are the numbers that count.

Practice sim numbers come from a fast approximate simulator the lab uses to scout habits: which four get picked, who leads, what moves open the game. Good for reading tendencies, but hypotheses only — a practice-sim number is never proof and is always labelled.

Confidence chips grade the verified sample: Strong evidence means 500+ verified games against 8+ different opponents; Established 200+ against 6+; Still developing 60+ against 3+; Early anything less. “Scouting probe out of date” means the habit data was recorded before the latest rules update — the verified record itself is always current.

A “game” is counted from this team’s side of a battle. “Picked” means chosen among the four Pokémon actually brought to a battle — teams register six, only four play.

How to read this report

Two kinds of evidence appear here, and they are not equal.

A “game” is counted from this team’s side of a battle. “Picked” means chosen among the four Pokémon actually brought to a battle — teams register six, only four play.
