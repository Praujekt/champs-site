PROVISIONAL. These are M-C candidates tested by the corrected incumbent against a declared opponent panel. No rating is published and no strength claim is made.

  • 15 candidates, 240 qualified native games.

  • Proposal material included 24 revalidated archive teams from 6 source families. Their historical scores guided selection only and are not shown as M-C results.
  • No ratings. Ratings stay off until the player that produced these games is qualified. A blank rating is a decision, not a gap in the data.
  • An M-B result cannot become a current M-C standing. The M-B board remains available and is not republished here. Open the Regulation M-B board — it is a different regulation and a different player, kept as history.
