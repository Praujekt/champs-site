What this board is, and what it is not

PROVISIONAL. These are M-C candidates tested by the corrected incumbent against a declared opponent panel. No rating is published and no strength claim is made.

  • 155 candidates, 2480 qualified native games.
  • Player champions-incumbent-open-sheet-v1, information mode open-sheet, lineage 7428a95979e1. This incumbent sees open sheets: it is not playing under tournament information limits.
  • Opponents are a declared panel of 4 teams, selected before any candidate existed. Two are fixed references in every generation and two rotate (one revalidated historical archetype, one of the ladder's own survivors), so 10 distinct opponents have been faced across 31 committed generation(s). Only the reference record is comparable across generations: a 10–0 against one rotating pair is not better than 8–2 against another.
  • No ratings. Ratings stay off until the player that produced these games is qualified. A blank rating is a decision, not a gap in the data.
  • An M-B result cannot become a current M-C standing. The M-B board remains available and is not republished here. Open the Regulation M-B board — it is a different regulation and a different player, kept as history.
